Typeset is lettering: balloons, captions, SFX and every word on the page, designed as part of the picture. (`letter` is an alias.)

## Lettering is voice

- **Balloon shape is character.** Round for ordinary speech; angular or spiked for shouting and menace; wavering for weakness; a dashed border for whisper; a cloud or borderless float for thought; a box for narration. Black balloons with white text when the line outweighs the art. A series' special voices (a masked speaker's perfect circle with a straight tail; an AI's hairline-bordered balloon) live in STYLE.md and are never improvised.
- **Face per voice, not one comic font for everything.** Dialogue face, caption face, a handwritten face for notes and asides, a display face for titles and calling cards; SFX are drawn in the scene's own hand. Fonts must be licensed for print and web; record them in STYLE.md.
- **Translated or in-world language** follows the series' marking rule (e.g. translated Bahasa in ⟨angle brackets⟩), consistently, every instance.

## Placement

- Balloons sit in the panel's lettering zone (the plan marks it opposite the subject); never over a face, a hand doing something, or the action's line.
- Reading order: balloons read in the series' direction, top to bottom, and in panel order; a balloon that straddles two panels belongs to the transition between them.
- Tails point to the speaker's mouth (or off-panel toward them); one tail per balloon; linked balloons for one speaker's beats.
- Leave margin inside balloons (text not touching the border) and room for 30% translation growth.

## Load

At most about 25 words per balloon, 40 per panel, three balloons per panel; small panels hold a word or a reaction. Over budget → split across panels, move to a talk tier, or `distill` the lines (with the user's approval).

## SFX

Drawn, not typed; sized to the sound; placed along the motion; allowed to cross borders and gutters (footsteps lettered in the gutter, a shout breaking the top edge). One dominant SFX per beat. Impact pages letter the hit unless the silence is deliberate and noted.

## Captions and titles

Captions for time, place and inner voice only; never to restate the picture. Chapter titles and calling cards are designed objects in the series' world, not text boxes.

## Verify

Detector `letter-*` families clear or justified; proof pages read in one order; the skeleton test passes (the page still reads with text removed).
