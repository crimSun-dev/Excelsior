Harden makes a chapter survive production and real-world editions: translation, flipping, platform limits, censorship variants, reprints.

## Translation and lettering room

- Budget text expansion: English → Bahasa Indonesia, Spanish, French or German commonly runs 15–35% longer; Japanese → English changes balloon shape (vertical to horizontal). Every balloon needs room to grow; a panel with no free area beside its balloon is a P1 for the localisation edition.
- SFX: keep plot-relevant SFX drawn on a separable layer, or leave space for a translation gloss beside it. Never bake a pun that carries plot into art with no alternative.
- Names, honorifics and in-world text (signs, screens, cards): one source of truth in SERIES.md; mark in-world text that must be translated in the beats (`"kind": "sign"`).
- Speech in another language within the story (e.g. translated Bahasa lettered in angle brackets): record the marking rule in STYLE.md and audit every instance.

## Reading direction and flipping

- If an edition will be flipped, list what must not mirror: written text, clocks and watches, handedness that matters to plot, asymmetrical costumes, logos, scars, a car's driving side. Mark those panels for redraw rather than flip.
- If the edition keeps the original direction for a different-direction market, plan an orientation page and keep the first spread's cues strong.

## Platform and rating variants

- Webtoon slice seams, file sizes, width; print trim and binding loss (see [print.md](print.md), [webtoon.md](webtoon.md)).
- Rating variants: identify panels that would need alternate framing for a stricter edition (violence, nudity, smoking, alcohol by region), and plan an alternate shot now, not a black bar later.

## Edge cases in the story

- Long names in captions and titles; the longest character name in the longest language in the smallest balloon.
- Recap or "previously" needs for serialised readers who missed a week (see [onboard.md](onboard.md)).
- Continuity across chapters: props, injuries, costumes, time of day.
