# Webtoon (vertical scroll) format

- **Canvas:** commonly 800 px wide (check the platform); slices up to the platform's height limit (often 1280 px per image, sometimes more); keep vital art and balloons off slice seams.
- **The gap is the clock:** about 0.05× width for action, 0.18× normal, 0.4–0.6× a held breath. Black gaps are rare (9% in Link Click); a black gap is an event.
- **Blocks:** mix tall blocks (median about 0.9× width) with tiny inserts; one block per beat; a reveal sits below the longest gap.
- **Episode shape:** the first screen re-anchors (place, face, question); the last screen hooks; episode length set by the platform's norms.
- **Lettering:** sized for a phone at arm's length; balloons inside blocks, or floating in the gap only when the gap is part of the beat; never across a seam.
- **Colour:** most webtoons are full colour; colour carries time and mood across the scroll; see [colorize.md](colorize.md).
- **Reading direction:** top to bottom; within a block, the series' horizontal direction.
