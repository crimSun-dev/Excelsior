Optimize tunes reading speed and production cost. (`pace` is an alias.) A chapter is slow when density and words drag; it is too fast when big pages fly by without story; it is expensive when the plan asks for more drawing than the team has.

## Reading speed

- **Too slow:** talk pages over the word budget, plateaus of dense pages, captions restating pictures. → `distill`, talk tiers, reaction pages.
- **Too fast:** splash inflation, thin pages of one or two panels with no story weight, action with no geography. → merge, add the establishing wide, give the peak a set-up.
- Measure: panels per page and words per page across the chapter (detector `--stats`), against the library's 4-panel centre and the mode.

## Production cost

- Count by panel: backgrounds (new vs reused), crowd scenes, vehicles and mechanical detail, full-figure action, colour pages, spreads.
- Reuse with intent: an establishing background reused at returns is a motif; a reused close-up is a cheat readers notice.
- Spend detail where the reader looks: the dominant panel gets the full render; small panels and inserts can be simpler.
- Record a per-page cost estimate (S/M/L) in the beats note and flag pages above the team's capacity in SERIES.md.

## Generation cost (when panels are image-generated)

- Reuse character prompt fragments; generate at the plan's crop size; never generate text into images; batch by scene so lighting stays consistent.

## Verify

Before and after: words per page, panels per page, splash count, estimated drawing load. Nothing the script owns was cut without approval.
