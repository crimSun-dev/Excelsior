Delight adds the small touches readers screenshot and share: the details that reward attention and make the series feel loved.

## Where delight lives

- **Background gags** that never interrupt the main read (a sign that changes between chapters, a cat that keeps appearing).
- **Motifs and rhymes:** an image that opens a chapter and closes the next; a framing reused at a turning point.
- **Title pages and chapter covers** designed as objects from the series' world (a match programme, a broadcast lower-third, a school notice).
- **Omake and chapter-break pages:** a four-panel gag, a character profile card, a map.
- **Balloon voice moments:** a single line lettered unforgettably at the right beat.
- **Easter eggs for veterans** (Rina): a callback the first-time reader doesn't need and the veteran won't forget.

## Rules

- Delight never costs clarity: if a gag competes with the story beat, move it to a quieter panel or cut it.
- In the series' grammar only: chibi, sweat drops and comic asides only where the series has established them.
- Canon-safe: no delight that implies a relationship, a reveal or a fact before its time.
- Scarcity: one or two per chapter land; ten become noise.

## Verify

List each touch with its page and the reader it rewards. Run the detuned read: remove all of them; the chapter must still work.
