# Sequence: deeper Follow and Converse guidance

## Follow (action, sport, chases)

- **Block it first.** Before panels, write the blocking in one paragraph: who starts where, who moves where, what hits what, where the camera is. If you can't write it, the reader can't read it.
- **Screen direction.** Keep each fighter on their side of the line; cross the line only on a cut the reader can follow (a wide, a reaction, a beat of reorientation).
- **Rhythm:** quick cuts (small slanted panels, 4–6 on the page), then one big swing (the largest diagonal frame for the blow); a page of aftermath (wide, pull back) after a big hit.
- **Cause and effect across the turn:** the wind-up at the bottom of a right page, the impact after the turn.
- **Sport:** the play has a geography (court, pitch, ring). Establish it once per scene with a high or bird's-eye wide; return to it after every big play so the score and positions stay legible.
- **Library:** DBS zig-zag bands, DB grid-and-diagonal, P5 angular cuts with reaction slivers.

## Converse (dialogue, comedy, romance, exposition)

- **Shots:** mediums and two-shots carry the scene; over-the-shoulder for confrontation; close-ups spent on the reaction that matters; an insert for the object that changes the conversation.
- **Talk tiers:** a narrow tier of small panels where lines live, beside a larger frame where the acting lives.
- **Subtext:** the line says one thing, the picture another; give the picture the room.
- **The 180° line:** keep speakers on their sides; balloon order follows.
- **Comedy:** strict grid, set-up in the first tiers, punch in the last; the reaction beat after the punch is its own panel; a sudden chibi or flat background marks the joke only where the series uses it.
- **Romance and tenderness:** take the frame away for the beat; slow the gutters; let a silence panel sit.
- **Exposition:** stage it as action (someone doing something while they explain), or as a device the reader enjoys (a diagram, a broadcast); never a page of talking heads.
- **Library:** HNK float and ornament for the uncanny tender beat, KSB reaction faces and straddling shouts, DB's still talk grid that makes its action pages hit.
