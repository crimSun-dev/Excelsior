Clarify fixes the words and the reading order so the reader cannot misread the scene: dialogue, captions, balloon order, who is speaking, what just happened.

## Diagnose

- **Who said that?** Tails ambiguous, off-panel speakers unmarked, two characters with the same balloon voice in one panel.
- **What order?** Balloons that read out of order across a tier; a reply placed above its question.
- **What happened?** Action that exists only in a caption; cause and effect split across a page turn with no visual link.
- **Jargon and lore dumps:** terms the reader hasn't been taught; a caption explaining what the picture should show.
- **Over-long lines** (`letter-balloon-long`, `letter-panel-dense`).

## Fix

- Re-place balloons for order; split a long line into two balloons in reading order; give an off-panel speaker a tail to the border and a voice.
- Move information from captions into pictures (a close-up of the object, a reaction) and from pictures that are too small into frames that can hold it.
- Teach terms in context: show the thing, then name it.
- Tighten lines only with the writer's approval; you may propose cuts, never silently change dialogue. Canon terms and names stay exact.

## Verify

Read the page once, fast, as Dan (new reader) and once as Tomas (letterer with 30% expansion). Neither should hesitate.
