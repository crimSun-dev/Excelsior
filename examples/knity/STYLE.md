# Style: K-nity (example record)

_From the Production Bible v2 house layer and the Manga Composition Standard v2. Confirm with `/excelsior document` once finished pages exist._

## World in one paragraph
Black-and-white ink with screentone, confident brush line, heavy spot blacks, clean white space, cinematic composition.

## Paper
Black paper for 2045, amber for the tower glow, white for the present. Gutters are tinted with the static colour in Chapter 2.

## Line
Confident brush line weight; heavy spot blacks.

## Tone and value
Spot-black strategy on 2045 pages; paper-light on present-day talk pages.

## Borders and gutters
Thin gutters, wider between tiers. One borderless beat per scene, for the most tender or charged moment.

## Page grammar
Reference-inspired layouts (mangaforge inspire); quick cuts, then one big swing on impact pages.

## Shot language
Talk and acting in medium shots; inserts kept for props.

## Lettering voices
- Ken's mask balloon: a perfect circle with a straight tail.
- Kkiri's balloon: a cyan-magenta hairline border.
- Calling cards: angular red-and-black boxes, two per chapter at most.
- Translated Bahasa: ⟨angle brackets⟩.

## Motifs and devices
- Silence is lettered as an empty page: the 3.2-second silences and 02:47 use void pages and strip pages with widening gaps.
- The opening card (black paper, one hairline, one tick, "ALTERNATE WORLD · 2045") returns to close Chapter 2.

## Format rules
Print, left-to-right, 7×10 in planning trim, 0.125 in bleed.

## Exceptions and open questions
- Pages 9, 25, 27, 47, 57 and 63 of Chapter 1's v3 plan promote the hit to the "big" size but the borrowed layout gives a different panel more area (detector: craft-peak-not-biggest).
